# Output

How the particles are drawn. Unlike the other stages, the Output node's main settings are on the **node itself** rather than in blocks.

## Render as

| Mode | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **billboard** | A flat quad always facing the camera. The default, and right for most things. |
| **stretched** | Stretched along its direction of travel. Sparks, rain, streaks, anything fast. |
| **mesh** | Draws a 3D model per particle. Debris, coins, rocks. Needs a **Particle Mesh** block. |
| **point** | A small billboard. |
| **trail** | Falls back to a stretched billboard in the preview, with a note saying so. A true ribbon renderer does not exist yet. |

**Stretched only works if particles have velocity.** On a system with no movement it looks identical to a billboard.

## Blending

| Mode | What it does |
|---|---|
| **additive** | Adds light to whatever is behind. Fire, sparks, magic, glows — most VFX. Black is invisible, so an additive sprite needs no alpha channel at all. |
| **alpha** | Ordinary transparency. Smoke, dust, debris, decals — anything dark, because additive cannot draw something darker than its background. |
| **premultiplied** | One texture holding both glow and smoke: colour above zero with alpha at zero is pure light. Unity calls it Premultiply, Niagara AlphaComposite. |
| **opaque** | No blending, depth write on. |

**If a dark effect is invisible, it is on additive.** Smoke is the usual case — switch it to alpha.

## Sorting

| Mode | When |
|---|---|
| **none** | Additive and premultiplied are order-independent, so this is correct and free. The default. |
| **depth** | Alpha blending usually needs it, or two puffs drawn in the wrong order show a hard edge. |
| **age** | Sort by particle age instead. |

Sorting costs CPU per frame. Use `depth` on alpha-blended systems and leave it off elsewhere.

## Blocks

| Block | Properties | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Sprite Texture** | `texture`, `blackPoint` (0) | The particle's image. Without one it draws with a built-in soft blob. |
| **Sprite Sheet** | `columns` (4), `rows` (4) | Says how the texture is divided into frames. Pair it with **Play Sprite Sheet** in Update. |
| **Particle Mesh** | `mesh` | The model to draw, for `mesh` mode. |

## Choosing a sprite

Click the texture slot to pick an image from your library, or generate one — see [Sprite Generation](/wiki/52).

**Black point** pulls the darkest part of a sprite down to nothing. A generated image is often *nearly* black rather than exactly black, and additive blending adds that remainder once per overlapping particle, which reads as a faint grey haze. Raise it until the haze clears; too far and the sprite loses its dimmest edges. A sprite whose background is exactly black needs none of this.

## HDR and brightness

Colours above 1 are allowed and are how a bright core blows out through the tonemapper rather than clipping flat. Set them in **Set Colour**, or as the brightness on a gradient stop.

The preview tone maps exactly like every other viewport in the app, so what you see is what the effect looks like in context.

## Cost

The Output stage is where an effect gets expensive, and the cost is **overdraw** — not the particle count. Sixty large transparent quads stacked on the same pixels cost far more than six hundred small ones spread out.

If an effect is heavy, reduce the *size* before the count.

The diagnostics strip estimates overdraw and the stats window shows `draws`. Systems that share a texture, blend mode, render mode and sheet layout are drawn together, so a four-system explosion is usually two or three draw calls rather than four.

## In the engines

Billboard, stretched and mesh modes, all four blend modes, both sort modes and flipbooks are native in Unity.

`point` becomes a small billboard, and `trail` becomes a stretched billboard — which is what the preview does too, so no fidelity is lost relative to what you saw.

One caveat is reported per import: a gradient stop with a **brightness above 1** is folded into an LDR colour, because Unity's Gradient has no HDR channel. Use the material's emission for the glow instead.
